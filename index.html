INTRODUCTION

About Me

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at SNIST and a PhD Research Scholar at SR University.

My academic interests include programming, machine learning, deep learning, and big-data technologies. My current doctoral research focuses on a hybrid mobile quantum-inspired convolutional neural network for intrusion detection in big-data environments using MapReduce.

Ongoing PhD ResearchRESEARCH AREA · CYBERSECURITY

Hybrid Mobile Quantum-Inspired Convolutional Neural Networks (MQuCNN) for Intrusion Detection in Big Data Environments using MapReduce

The research investigates a hybrid intrusion detection framework that combines MobileNet and a quantum-inspired convolutional network (QuCNet), supported by distributed big-data processing.
